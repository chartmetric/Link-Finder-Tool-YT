name: Correction rerun safety
description: Approval boundary between full musician attribution correction, ordinary discovery and diagnostic benchmarking.
---

Full Tier 1–5 musician attribution-correction reruns require a separately approved
benchmark policy and complete, fresh results bound to exact models, request schema,
source evidence and unchanged acceptance rules. Preparation-only benchmarks,
untested scorecards and verifier-only experiments cannot authorize them.

**Why:** The user explicitly separated future correction readiness from the
ongoing investigation of individual verifier decisions and prohibited choosing
new thresholds or altering live links while preparing the guard.

**How to apply:** Keep ordinary research, no-match discovery, brands and cricket
outside this approval requirement. Never repurpose their executors or remove
pilot spending limits to launch a correction. Preserve a separate paid-run
approval, original conservative cumulative reservations, and claims.record /
normal resolver ingestion only. Changes to the bound model, prompts, evidence
or acceptance code invalidate the readiness approval; a benchmark receipt is
not a live account approval.
