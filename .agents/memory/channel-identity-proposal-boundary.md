---
name: Channel identity proposal boundary
description: Why channel-identity safeguards must remain separate from executable representation policies until approved.
---

Prepare channel-identity safeguards as a separately versioned offline contract,
not a silent change to an existing representation proposal.

**Why:** Representation proposals are also consumed by executable review paths.
Changing their defaults can activate new policy even when the helper describes
itself as experimental. The user requested preparation without policy activation
and preservation of historical decisions.

**How to apply:** Keep existing requests and response meanings unchanged until
explicit activation approval. Bind new answers to the exact new request and
keep source-based holds separate from model choices. Collaboration credits
establish recording participation, not identity of every credited artist's
channel. Supported Topic music presence is still in scope, not artist management.
