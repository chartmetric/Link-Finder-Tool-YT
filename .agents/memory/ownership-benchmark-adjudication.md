---
name: Ownership benchmark adjudication
description: Independence and exposure limits for source-reviewed artist ownership examples
---

Keep source-reviewed ownership labels separate from user corrections, music-ID
connections, verifier judgments and unresolved candidates.

**Why:** Exact catalog music links can belong to labels, venues or fans; they
establish a connection, not ownership. Source-reviewed examples drawn from prior
development experiments are already exposed and cannot honestly become a held-out
calibration set. Independent source review does not imply two human adjudicators.

**How to apply:** Preserve original source observation times (unknown stays
unknown), keep grading conclusions outside verifier requests, and report model
holds separately from missing exact-link evidence. Before interpreting results,
identify the label tier, reused accounts and prior exposure. No population or
threshold claim follows from this convenience sample.

Do not turn an ownership answer into a source-trust declaration for an acceptance gate.

**Why:** A reviewed artist website can support a benchmark ownership label without
providing an independently captured catalog-profile declaration of that website.
Inventing that missing declaration to exercise a new acceptance route would make
the evaluation circular even if no answer label appeared in the model request.

**How to apply:** Keep grading evidence separate from acceptance-input construction,
not just verifier input. Missing automatic domain-trust evidence must remain
missing. Synthetic trust packets can test rule behavior but cannot qualify real
benchmark artists or establish production readiness.

Keep website source trust scoped to the declared page, not account-URL aliases
or the shared domain.

**Why:** Website queries and fragments can select different artists on the same
host. URL equivalence suitable for music account IDs can therefore transfer
source trust to the wrong artist's page.

**How to apply:** Preserve the complete normalized website URL across acquisition,
evidence joins and acceptance checks. Do not reuse account-specific URL cleanup
to decide that two website observations are the same evidence source.
