---
name: Ownership benchmark adjudication
description: Independence and exposure limits for source-reviewed artist ownership examples
---

Keep source-reviewed ownership labels separate from user corrections, music-ID
connections, verifier judgments and unresolved candidates.

**Why:** Exact catalog music links can belong to labels, venues or fans; they
establish a connection, not ownership. Source-reviewed examples drawn from prior
development experiments are already exposed and cannot honestly become a held-out
calibration set. Independent source review does not imply two human adjudicators.

**How to apply:** Preserve original source observation times (unknown stays
unknown), keep grading conclusions outside verifier requests, and report model
holds separately from missing exact-link evidence. Before interpreting results,
identify the label tier, reused accounts and prior exposure. No population or
threshold claim follows from this convenience sample.

User-feedback diagnostics must compare raw verifier assessments separately from
production safeguards that already know the user's rejections.

**Why:** Known rejected artist/channel pairs are also recorded in the production
safety policy. Scoring the final guarded outcome would credit the verifier for
answers supplied by that policy. Batch-level “looks correct” feedback also does
not establish individually adjudicated channel management.

**How to apply:** Keep feedback outside verifier evidence, report its strength
and historical scope, and count uncertain answers separately from rejections.
Describe agreement with user feedback, not ownership accuracy or held-out
calibration. A later model stage needs its own exact approval.

Do not substitute video-uploader information for a full channel capture in a
reviewed-example comparison.

**Why:** The reviewed correct examples had saved channel captures, while the
rejected examples only had video-level uploader records. Treating those as
equivalent would confound the test with missing evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction and obtain explicit capture approval
for missing channel information. Diagnostic captures of intentionally incorrect
pairs must remain transport-only: keep billing, resource-ID, replay and uncertain
request protections, but never register those pairs as artist/channel ownership
claims. Missing current eligibility is a deferral, not a reason to weaken
campaign checks; shrink both sides when necessary to retain a balanced sample.

Cross-artist synthetic challenges are not independently source-reviewed negative
ownership labels, even when catalog IDs differ or saved model answers disagree.

**Why:** The user requires control-selection diagnostics to remain separate from
benchmark-policy approval; rotating saved accounts cannot supply missing
ownership adjudication or calibrate false-positive rates.

**How to apply:** Report account reuse and diversity shortages explicitly, keep
ownership labels unassigned, and require the separately approved benchmark policy
before scoring accuracy.

Do not turn an ownership answer into a source-trust declaration for an acceptance gate.

**Why:** A reviewed artist website can support a benchmark ownership label without
providing an independently captured catalog-profile declaration of that website.
Inventing that missing declaration to exercise a new acceptance route would make
the evaluation circular even if no answer label appeared in the model request.

**How to apply:** Keep grading evidence separate from acceptance-input construction,
not just verifier input. Missing automatic domain-trust evidence must remain
missing. Synthetic trust packets can test rule behavior but cannot qualify real
benchmark artists or establish production readiness.

Keep website source trust scoped to the declared page, not account-URL aliases
or the shared domain.

**Why:** Website queries and fragments can select different artists on the same
host. URL equivalence suitable for music account IDs can therefore transfer
source trust to the wrong artist's page.

**How to apply:** Preserve the complete normalized website URL across acquisition,
evidence joins and acceptance checks. Do not reuse account-specific URL cleanup
to decide that two website observations are the same evidence source.

No general current-ownership validity interval has been approved. Treat original
ownership observations as historical-only rather than inventing a default TTL.
Expiry and suspected handle reuse are uncertainty about currency, not evidence
that the historical identity was wrong.

**Why:** Handles can be repurposed, and this project has not authorized a periodic
evidence-collection budget or a universal validity window.

**How to apply:** Require an explicit, explained re-adjudication window for
current grading; retain historical answers separately. Renewing grading evidence
does not renew frozen verifier inputs or authorize new collection. Even a
zero-cost source refresh needs separate scope and spending approval.

A two-reviewer requirement is an exclusion policy, not evidence that a second
review has happened. Do not manufacture agreement by copying the original curator,
using verifier scores, or counting revisions as additional votes.

**Why:** The historical source decisions were made by a single curator; changing
governance must not retroactively misrepresent their independence.

**How to apply:** Keep missing second reviews explicit and unscored until a real,
separately recorded reviewer decision is available. Governance changes alone do
not authorize paid verification or threshold calibration.
