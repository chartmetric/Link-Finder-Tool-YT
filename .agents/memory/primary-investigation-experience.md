---
name: Primary investigation experience
description: Product priority and historical-data expectations for individual-artist investigations.
---

Prioritize single-URL artist investigations as the primary experience, keep the existing catalog accessible separately, and show shared saved investigations below the input.

**Why:** The user requested the single-artist flow as the primary page and chose single-artist search history rather than the existing catalog or approved suggestions for the table.

**How to apply:** Do not substitute catalog records for investigation history or claim older searches were saved. History begins with newly completed investigations; a stored search snapshot is not approval of candidate links.
