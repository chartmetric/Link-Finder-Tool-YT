---
name: Verification evidence audits
description: Distinguish original verifier inputs from reconstructions and synthetic fixtures.
---

Do not attribute an archived model result to a request reconstructed with a newer serializer. Keep source-path availability, account ownership, model disagreement and application acceptance separate.

**Why:** Historical completion packets can lack outgoing input bytes, while subsequent question changes and provenance repairs change both evidence interpretation and request size. A complete music-ID path does not establish how the historical model received it or prove that a wording repair fixes its score.

**How to apply:** Prefer archived request bodies and hashes when available; explicitly label reconstructions otherwise. Measure the whole serialized request including every candidate and typed question against the existing byte cap. Fixture decisions test the acceptance contract, not model accuracy. When isolating simultaneous verdict and score failures with artificial answers, keep real outputs alongside the counterfactuals; structurally eligible cases are not recovered or verified links. A new paid evaluation always needs its own approval and exclusive journal.

A completed research workflow with no evaluated holdouts has not passed an
ownership quality check.

**Why:** A generic batch can complete an empty regression stage successfully
while every discovered candidate remains unsupported. Operational completion
must not become authorization for attribution corrections or imports.

**How to apply:** Require an explicit scored benchmark record for quality
readiness. Keep research-only workflow completion, candidate discovery,
source-supported attribution and claims eligibility as separate results.

Preserve explicit account declarations and exact alternate-platform catalog
paths ahead of general metadata when fitting verifier requests. A path to the
artist's frozen YouTube, X or TikTok account need not pass through Spotify.
Profile/platform snapshots remain contextual even when they name a handle.

**Why:** Bulk-run reviews exposed plausible accounts held because the verifier
received only a Spotify reference, while corroborating catalog links and saved
declarations were unused or dropped to fit duplicated question text. Finding
candidates and delivering their evidence are separate success criteria.

**How to apply:** Normalize compatibility Unicode in platform-labeled handles;
keep provenance and age; deduplicate prompts before omitting low-priority facts.
Record omissions and hold explicitly if essential evidence cannot fit. Do not
claim archived decisions improved without a separately approved model rerun.

Keep observed search layouts separate from adversarial mutations of saved
results. A locale query or several named artists in a caption is not a
reviewed owner header or evidence of coauthorship.

**Why:** Saved search packets contain localized separators and mixed-account
content, but do not necessarily contain every ownership failure being tested.
Calling derived threat cases real captures would overstate the evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve scrubbed formatting provenance; label mutations
explicitly and leave unsupported owner layouts unconfirmed. Fixture coverage
alone does not authorize expanding supported owner formats.

Keep repeated artist/account observations distinct from independent sample size.

**Why:** A favorable replay can coexist with another held or conflicting occurrence
for the same account; selecting only that replay hides disagreement and inflates
apparent coverage. Cross-artist account reuse is an unresolved ownership risk,
not an automatic identity adjudication.

**How to apply:** Retain occurrence-level decisions alongside pair-level summaries,
identify the aggregation rule, and report distinct artists, accounts and pairs.
Never describe an offline source-policy acceptance as a fresh model result or a
production-approved correction.

Keep recorded model scores unchanged when an evidence-based research policy
finds additional support. Show the proposed support route separately from the
historical score and verdict.

**Why:** The user found correct candidates held below 0.90. A policy simulation
can change candidate support without changing what the model actually answered;
silently upgrading the saved score or calling that a fresh verification would
misrepresent the experiment.

Keep account-owned post clues distinct from third-party release mentions and
related content, even when saved profile bios are incomplete. A release title
or hashtag match supplies consistency context, never ownership proof.

**Why:** The user requested recovery of omitted post evidence without weakening
the ownership threshold or conflating fans, covers, mashups and collaborators
with the artist.

**How to apply:** Preserve search-source attribution and date uncertainty; do not
describe search snippets as newly fetched posts. Any separately approved model
evaluation must distinguish evidence delivery from ownership accuracy.

Removing prior workflow identity labels is input hygiene, not evidence of
improved recall. Keep source-resolution states and explicit ownership uncertainty
separate from those prior identity judgments.

**Why:** The previous two-artist label-removal experiment did not improve
acceptance; a cleaner request alone does not demonstrate better decisions.

**How to apply:** Exclude workflow identity status/reasons consistently across
normal and compact inputs without rewriting archived requests or decisions.
Do not infer permission for paid reruns, new ownership criteria, threshold
changes or link imports from this hygiene rule.

Historical "facts-only" ablations are not the current source-only contract:
their intentional source-field omissions must remain reproducible. A newly
assembled baseline is not an archived historical baseline.

**Why:** Reusing an old ablation to build fresh inputs removes resolution and
unknown-ownership evidence along with workflow labels, confounding the hygiene
change with a different evidence set.

**How to apply:** Give fresh source-only construction a separate, field-specific
path. Frozen transports must verify and preserve admitted bytes, not sanitize
them after hashing. Label synthetic historical-contract fixtures as synthetic,
not as archived captures.

Missing saved evidence-delivery counts mean unknown, never zero. Per-candidate
scan diagnostics may be taken from original archived request packets, but must
not be recomputed with today's matcher to describe a historical decision.

**Why:** Older pilot archives predate omission diagnostics. A model hold does not
tell us whether its request received all discovered post evidence.

**How to apply:** Show unavailable counts explicitly; distinguish saved result
audits from original request diagnostics and keep both separate from verdicts.

Supplemental saved catalog metadata does not inherit the archived discovery
batch's spending authorization.

**Why:** The user requires verification-only reuse of archived candidates, with
explicit spending approval even when the metadata is already available.

**How to apply:** Prepare offline first, bind new approval to the exact saved
evidence and candidate scope, preserve historical packets and source dates, and
retain non-replayable admissions. Never repeat discovery or enable experimental
acceptance as a side effect of supplementing evidence.
