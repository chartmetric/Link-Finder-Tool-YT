---
name: Cross-platform follower counts
description: Identity-verification tradeoff for comparing audiences across social platforms.
---

Use a large follower-count gap as negative identity evidence when both counts are available, but never as a hard rejection on its own.

**Why:** A similarly named account with 200,000 followers and another with 200 may be unrelated, but legitimate creators can have dramatically different audiences across platforms. Direct official cross-links and other strong identity evidence should still be able to outweigh a gap.

**How to apply:** Include the disparity and both counts in human-readable reasons, avoid assuming missing counts are zero, and keep the search/discovery score distinct from an identity verdict.
