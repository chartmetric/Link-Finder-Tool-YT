# YouTube artist-ownership checks

The YouTube policy is separate from Instagram's fingerprinted live executor.
It does not start searches, fetch channels, invoke models, or change catalog links.
It can evaluate saved channel captures and exact-input model answers.

## Behavior

- Catalog identity must bind profile ID and Chartmetric artist ID.
- Search titles and snippets remain discovery evidence, never channel biographies.
- Exact returned channel IDs and handles establish aliases; names do not.
- Topic/auto-generated, fan, organizational, and artist-owned channels are distinct.
- Topic channels may carry an artist's music without being artist-owned.
- A badge, name, subscriber count, or label affiliation alone does not prove ownership.
- A label-owning artist is not automatically classified as a label account.
- Large audience gaps are caution, not automatic rejection.
- Recorded `likely` judgments have no arbitrary 0.9 cutoff, but must match the
  exact evidence fingerprint and resolve the channel as artist-owned.
- Reused channels and conflicting music artist IDs require review.
- Multiple supported artist channels may coexist without being called aliases.
- A captured declaration from an exact catalog account plus consistent channel
  self-identification can support a review suggestion without a model.
- All outputs remain review suggestions, never automatic catalog acceptance.
- No channel result is a completed search assessment, not proof of no account.

## Offline saved-batch review

`node scripts/src/review-saved-youtube.mjs`

Reads the frozen 20-artist YouTube discovery archive. Supplemental catalog
metadata is read-only and saved independently of the original results.
The review preserves all candidates and writes ownership findings, the precise
metadata supplied, and prepared verification inputs to an additive review folder.
Existing catalog evidence can be reused after an interrupted offline review.
Completed review outputs are not overwritten.

Optional channel captures and answers are read from `channel-captures` and
`channel-answers` inside the frozen batch directory, keyed by the SHA-256 digest
of the JSON-serialized candidate URL. Their absence is explicit.

## Channel capture contract

A packet has `status: 200`, `requestedUrl`, `capturedAt`, `body`, and
`bodySha256` (SHA-256 of JSON.stringify(body)). Body fields include `channelId`,
`handle`, `url`, `displayName`, `description`, `externalUrls`, `subscribers`,
`isAutoGenerated`, `isOfficialArtistChannel`, and captured videos when available.
The requested URL must bind to the returned identifiers; conflicting or absent
bindings cannot qualify.

Recorded answers have `inputSha256` equal to the prepared verification input and
`answer: {verdict, sameIdentityProbability, channelType, reasons}`. The supported
channel types are artist_owned, topic, fan, organization, unrelated, and unknown.
Future collectors must preserve raw receipts and normalize fields without
inventing missing values. These are evidence contracts, not a live provider runner.

## Paid execution

The earlier discovery authorization was discovery-only. This policy does not
extend that approval to channel collection or model calls. Any paid execution
needs a separately frozen scope and explicit authorization, with all historical
charges retained and uncertain admissions never replayed. Do not alter or borrow
the live Instagram campaign's spending allowance.

### Approved saved-20 ownership check

Preparation: `node scripts/src/youtube-ownership-check-prep.mjs`.
Offline preflight: `node scripts/src/run-youtube-ownership-check.mjs`.
The live command additionally requires `--run-approved`,
`--additional-cap-cents=136`, and `--plan-sha256=` with the exact saved plan
digest. Those flags do not themselves grant permission; an explicit user
approval for that frozen scope is required.

The approved check includes 34 channel-profile requests and up to 34 conditional
model requests. It preserves the original $0.40 conservative discovery
reservation. At two cents reserved per request, the additional allowance is
$1.36, with a maximum combined reservation of $1.76 under the unchanged $2
YouTube ceiling. Reservations are not provider invoices.

The two additive coordination tables, `youtube_ownership_checks` and
`youtube_ownership_admissions`, share the existing discovery parent budget.
Every admission locks that parent and the ownership phase, checks ownership,
checks the frozen request hash and approved operation, checks for unresolved
admissions, and increments both counters transactionally. The parent must
always equal its original reservation plus the phase's reservations. A unique
parent-phase binding prevents a second worker from resetting the allowance.
Original discovery results and catalog links are not updated.

An admission is never deleted or retried. Raw receipts are written before an
admission becomes returned. Funding, access/authentication, rate-limit,
unreadable-response, and uncertain-transport outcomes stop execution. An
archived unavailable-channel response or a response that cannot bind the
requested channel is missing evidence, not proof that no account exists.

The provider's documented channel response has `channelId`, `channel`, `name`,
`description`, `links`, and `subscriberCount`. Normalize only returned fields;
the documented HTTP YouTube canonical channel URL can become HTTPS on the
same allowed host. Do not invent a handle from the requested URL or treat a
generic badge as an Official Artist Channel badge. Keep the full raw receipt.

All channel captures are collected before ownership checks so reused channel
IDs and handle aliases can be assessed across the cohort. Known Topic, fan,
organizational, and conflicting channels do not consume model calls. Models
receive the full frozen artist context, full channel capture, search evidence,
and ownership rules. Requests above the approved 6,000-byte limit are held
explicitly; no biography or catalog context is silently cut. Freeze the exact
request before admission and bind typed answers to that request and policy
input. A low `likely` score is not vetoed solely by an arbitrary threshold.

Execution evidence and CSVs are saved additively under the frozen batch's
`ownership-check-execution` directory. None of these scripts changes the live
Instagram executor or its funding.

The requested JEV alias can return the concrete dated model name. Accept only
the exact observed release allowed by the transport and preserve both names;
an unrelated or unknown release still stops the phase. This is not a model
fallback.

An explicit `--continue-approved` recovery is supported only for a stopped,
drained phase with every admission reconciled to its local journal, exact
request and archived receipt. The transaction rotates the owner and seals a
new continuation authorization without changing either spending counter or
ceiling. Previously returned requests are reused from their receipts, not sent
again; immutable capture/input files must compare exactly before reuse. An
uncertain admission blocks this continuation.

### Offline proposal for the 13 oversized saved requests

Run `node scripts/src/youtube-ownership-size-prep.mjs` from the workspace root.
This reads local files only: no providers, database, credentials or live runner.
It writes an additive `ownership-size-preparation` directory and refuses to
overwrite an existing proposal. It accepts no run or approval flags.

The chosen approach is **unchanged full request bodies**, not a compact format.
The 13 held requests measure 6,305–11,744 UTF-8 bytes including rules and typed
questions. A proposed 11,744-byte ceiling is the smallest limit covering them
without shortening biographies, dropping catalog context, deduplicating source
observations or asking a model to decode a new representation. This changes the
allowance, not the evidence, model, questions or ownership standard. It does not
claim improved accuracy or any new verified matches.

The audit rebuilds source-only artist fields from the frozen catalog overlay,
compares the entire saved artist/search input, binds each normalized capture to
its full raw receipt, and compares every request, byte count and fingerprint.
Catalog envelope review flags and historical judgments are not request inputs.
Ten completed model answers must match their original evidence and outgoing
requests exactly; they are recorded separately and never scheduled again.
Every original file, including full raw captures, catalog context, admissions,
answers and outcomes, has a byte-level SHA-256 entry in the source manifest.
Those files stay in place and are checked again before and after output.

The archived spending snapshot preserves $1.28 already reserved ($0.40 discovery
plus $0.88 ownership). Thirteen model calls would reserve at most another $0.26,
giving $1.54 combined, within the unchanged $1.76 combined phase allowance and
$2 parent. These are reservations, not invoices. Larger requests can cost more;
the existing post-response cost check is not a provider-enforced price ceiling.
Pricing and the shared ledger must be reconciled before any paid launch.

`proposal.json` is **not authorization**. It contains the exact 13 bodies and
hashes, preserved completed bindings, source manifest, proposed allowance,
spending arithmetic and execution prerequisites. Explicit approval must bind to
its proposal hash and request scope. There is no new dispatch path: the transport
still rejects bodies over 6,000 bytes, and the existing stopped-phase recovery
does not reopen a completed phase. Any future approved continuation must retain
the original allocation, reconcile all admissions, fence the old worker and use
only these untouched operations, without a new budget or Instagram funding.
