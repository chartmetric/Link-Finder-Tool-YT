# YouTube artist-ownership checks

The YouTube policy is separate from Instagram's fingerprinted live executor.
It does not start searches, fetch channels, invoke models, or change catalog links.
It can evaluate saved channel captures and exact-input model answers.

## Behavior

- Catalog identity must bind profile ID and Chartmetric artist ID.
- Search titles and snippets remain discovery evidence, never channel biographies.
- Exact returned channel IDs and handles establish aliases; names do not.
- Topic/auto-generated, fan, organizational, and artist-owned channels are distinct.
- Topic channels may carry an artist's music without being artist-owned.
- A badge, name, subscriber count, or label affiliation alone does not prove ownership.
- A label-owning artist is not automatically classified as a label account.
- Large audience gaps are caution, not automatic rejection.
- Recorded `likely` judgments have no arbitrary 0.9 cutoff, but must match the
  exact evidence fingerprint and resolve the channel as artist-owned.
- Reused channels and conflicting music artist IDs require review.
- Multiple supported artist channels may coexist without being called aliases.
- A captured declaration from an exact catalog account plus consistent channel
  self-identification can support a review suggestion without a model.
- All outputs remain review suggestions, never automatic catalog acceptance.
- No channel result is a completed search assessment, not proof of no account.

## Offline saved-batch review

`node scripts/src/review-saved-youtube.mjs`

Reads the frozen 20-artist YouTube discovery archive. Supplemental catalog
metadata is read-only and saved independently of the original results.
The review preserves all candidates and writes ownership findings, the precise
metadata supplied, and prepared verification inputs to an additive review folder.
Existing catalog evidence can be reused after an interrupted offline review.
Completed review outputs are not overwritten.

Optional channel captures and answers are read from `channel-captures` and
`channel-answers` inside the frozen batch directory, keyed by the SHA-256 digest
of the JSON-serialized candidate URL. Their absence is explicit.

## Channel capture contract

A packet has `status: 200`, `requestedUrl`, `capturedAt`, `body`, and
`bodySha256` (SHA-256 of JSON.stringify(body)). Body fields include `channelId`,
`handle`, `url`, `displayName`, `description`, `externalUrls`, `subscribers`,
`isAutoGenerated`, `isOfficialArtistChannel`, and captured videos when available.
The requested URL must bind to the returned identifiers; conflicting or absent
bindings cannot qualify.

Recorded answers have `inputSha256` equal to the prepared verification input and
`answer: {verdict, sameIdentityProbability, channelType, reasons}`. The supported
channel types are artist_owned, topic, fan, organization, unrelated, and unknown.
Future collectors must preserve raw receipts and normalize fields without
inventing missing values. These are evidence contracts, not a live provider runner.

## Paid execution

The earlier discovery authorization was discovery-only. This policy does not
extend that approval to channel collection or model calls. Any paid execution
needs a separately frozen scope and explicit authorization, with all historical
charges retained and uncertain admissions never replayed. Do not alter or borrow
the live Instagram campaign's spending allowance.
