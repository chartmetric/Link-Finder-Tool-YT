# Single-artist investigation: executive summary

- **Purpose:** Starting from one known Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube profile URL, Find Social Links suggests possible accounts on the **other two platforms** for human review. The supplied account is the reference, never a returned candidate.
- **How discovery works:** The app reads the reference profile to obtain its display name and handle, then runs separate, concurrent, site-restricted Serper searches for the missing platforms. Gemini also searches for both using the original URL as an identity clue; ScrapeCreators can fill a missing platform when needed.
- **How matches are checked:** A rule-based comparison weighs names, handles, shared bio words, direct-link evidence, verified status, and available follower counts. Large follower-count gaps lower confidence but do not override strong identity evidence automatically. JEV separately evaluates candidates whose metadata can be read.
- **How to interpret results:** The **search score is a discovery-ranking heuristic, not an identity probability**. The page shows comparison reasons and JEV verdicts separately, prioritizes candidates with stronger verification evidence, and provides source links and provider statuses for inspection.
- **Reliability and safeguards:** Only profile-page URLs on the two target platforms are eligible. Failed metadata lookups or JEV decisions are reported rather than fabricated, and successfully discovered candidates remain visible when a later check fails. New investigations are saved to shared history below the input and can be reopened without another search; candidate links are not automatically approved or added to the official catalog.
- **Current limitations:** Image pixels are not compared; model verdicts and search results are not proof of ownership. Some YouTube channel-ID candidates cannot be resolved for verification, provider-level failures can lose otherwise successful sibling search results, and displayed costs are estimates rather than a complete bill.
